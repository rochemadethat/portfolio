// BIOGRAPHY

ROCHELLE "ROCHE" SACKS

VIDEO PRODUCER • EDITOR • CINEMATOGRAPHER • CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Hi! I’m Rochelle. As a seasoned video producer and editor, I’ve spent the past twelve years helping commercial, government and influencer brands develop digital storytelling that helps bolster their social media presence, elevate meaningful audience engagement and grow their impact online.

With experience creating platform-specific long and short video products for large clients (U.S. Department of State, Refinery29, influencers), I thrive both as a full-service video producer/editor, project manager, content creator, and communications strategist.

I also bring over ten years of experience working as a photographer, sound mixer/designer, international runway model and musician. Growing up overseas in Vietnam, Korea, and Panama, my international background empowers me to intersect my global and creative interests as a digital storyteller. My work is informed by the connections made with my subjects on a human level, understanding that behind every video are real stories waiting to be told.
